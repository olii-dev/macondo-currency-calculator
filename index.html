Hardware Funding

Parts grants are capped by level. Each dollar of funding converts to hours at your level's funding rate, and those hours are deducted from your ship before gold is calculated.

Hardware Funding vs Gold

On hardware build ships, your logged hours split between parts funding and gold. The funding rate depends on your project level:

  • L1: $4/hr (up to $100 cap)
  • L2: $4.50/hr (up to $200 cap)
  • L3: $5/hr (up to $400 cap)
  • L4: $6/hr (up to $1,000 cap)

Example: 20 hours at L3 with $20 funding → 4 funding hrs ($20 ÷ $5) + 16 gold hrs × 50 gold/hr = 800 base gold (before streak).

Reviewers sometimes round funding up to convenient tiers ($10, $20, $50, etc.). Use the checkbox in the calculator to estimate that — it's based on community reports, not official policy.

Starfruit

To purchase some shop items, you need to unlock them with a starfruit purchase. You earn starfruit when a reviewer marks your ship as "extra fruity" during approval.

  • Each extra fruity ship earns 1 starfruit in addition to normal gold
  • The flag is per ship, not per project which means every submission is a new opportunity to get starfruit
  • Spending 1 starfruit unlocks the gated shop item, but you still need the gold price to buy it
  • Unlocks are one time per item and stay permanent even if the item is edited
  • Starfruit cannot be converted to gold or traded

Streak System

Every project tracks its own project streak, the number of consecutive days you've put real work into that project.

  • Each streak day adds +1% to your gold when you ship ( for example 10 days = 1.10x, 30 days = 1.30x)
  • A streak day needs at least 1 hour total per day (Hackatime + journal hours combined)
  • Missing a day on a project resets that project's streak to 1, (unless you use a streak freeze) other projects are unaffected
  • Streak freezes (buy in the shop) auto spend (you can turn this off) to protect a missed day, maximum 5 at a time
  • Streak days run midnight to midnight in your timezone (your local timezone) and refresh in --

Pending Hardware Gold

Hardware projects gate their gold reward behind a build complete ship.

  • When a non build complete hardware ship is approved, gold is recorded but pending
  • Pending gold doesn't count toward your balance and can't be spent
  • Submit a "build complete" ship on the same project to unlock all pending gold at once
  • The pending amount shows on your profile
